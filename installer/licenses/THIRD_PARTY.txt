Notes
-----
- Qt for Python is offered under LGPL-3.0, GPL-2.0 or GPL-3.0; Callers Caddy
  uses it under LGPL-3.0. The LGPL-3.0 builds on the GPL-3.0, so both texts
  are included. Qt itself contains further third-party code, listed at
  https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html
- The Qt, FFmpeg and libsndfile libraries are shipped as separate DLL files
  and can be replaced by the user.
- Source code of the LGPL components: https://download.qt.io,
